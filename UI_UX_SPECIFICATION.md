# UI/UX Specification

## Design direction
Premium, elegant and trustworthy, with an Indian wedding visual language.

## Palette
- Emerald: #173D36
- Gold: #C18B55
- Ivory: #F8F5F0
- Neutral ink and borders

## Responsive behavior
- Mobile-first navigation and forms
- Two-column and three-column discovery layouts at larger widths
- Clear loading, empty and validation states

## Provisional brand
"Wedlock" and a text wordmark are placeholders only.
