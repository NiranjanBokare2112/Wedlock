# User Journeys

## Customer
1. Search by category and city.
2. Open a listing.
3. Post a requirement or begin a booking.
4. Receive matched leads and quotations.
5. Compare proposals and accept a quotation.
6. Continue to payment and transaction confirmation.
7. Track booking or order status.

## Vendor
1. Register and submit business documents.
2. Select categories and service areas.
3. Create listings and availability.
4. Receive leads.
5. Submit quotations.
6. Fulfill accepted bookings or seller orders.

## Administrator
1. Review pending vendors and listings.
2. Moderate public content.
3. Investigate disputes and refunds.
4. Review audit and financial records.
